I'm a first-year CS PhD student at CIS, University of Pennsylvania working under the supervision of Professor Jiatao Gu.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at Columbia College, Columbia University in New York with a degree in Mathematics and Computer Science. Research

UniLAP: A Unified Latent Action Policy for Cross-Embodiment Manipulation

Lei Yao, Qiao Feng, Mutian Tong, Qihao Yun, Lap-Pui Chau, Lingjie Liu

arXiv, 2026

project page / arXiv

UniLAP learns a continuous latent action policy from action-free videos using flow matching, enabling adaptation across embodiments without requiring future frames at inference.

Enabling Immersive Audio-Visual Experience from Any Video

Zitong Lan, Mutian Tong, Jiatao Gu, Mingmin Zhao

arXiv, 2026

arXiv / demo

OmniDream transforms a silent monocular video into an immersive panoramic experience with spatially aligned audio, using object-centric sound generation and physics-based acoustic simulation.

PointAction: 3D Points as Universal Action Representations for Robot Control

Mutian Tong*, Han Jiang*, Qiao Feng, Lingjie Liu, Jiatao Gu

arXiv, 2026

project page / arXiv

PointAction bridges the gap between generative video models and robust robot control by jointly predicting RGB frames and dynamic 3D pointmaps, which serve as a universal, geometry-aware action interface.

* Equal contribution.

Spatiotemporally Consistent Indoor Lighting Estimation with Diffusion Priors

Mutian Tong, Rundi Wu, Changxi Zheng

SIGGRAPH, 2025

project page / arXiv

A method for recovering dynamic 3D indoor lighting from a video by learning a light field with help from a 2D diffusion model.
